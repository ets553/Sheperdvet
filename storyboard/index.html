App storyboard · clickable prototype

Nestmatch

Real estate lead gen with a swipe. A six-question interview collects what the buyer wants and who to loop in, then a short thinking screen leads into a deck of matched homes to swipe on. After that, a household sees what everyone saved, the app learns what each member values, and buyers can rethink their goals.
